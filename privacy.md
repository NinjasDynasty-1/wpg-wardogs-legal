WPG WARDOGS PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WPG Wardogs and Wasted Prodigy Gamers ("WPG", "we", "our", or "us") collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the WPG Wardogs Discord bot.

By using WPG Wardogs, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. INFORMATION WE MAY COLLECT

WPG Wardogs may collect and store information necessary to provide bot features.

This may include:

DISCORD INFORMATION

- Discord user ID
- Discord username
- Discord display name
- Discord server ID
- Discord channel ID
- Discord role information when required for bot features
- Command usage related to WPG Wardogs

GAME AND LINKED ACCOUNT INFORMATION

When you voluntarily link an account, we may store:

- Steam ID
- Steam-linked identity
- WARDOGS player name
- Public WARDOGS career information
- Public leaderboard information

WPG SERVER STATISTICS

The bot may store statistics observed through WPG-operated server systems, including:

- Player name
- Steam ID
- Faction
- Kills
- Deaths
- Matches
- Wins
- Losses
- Playtime
- WPG experience
- WPG rank
- Server leaderboard position
- Match participation
- Other WPG server progression information

PUBLIC CAREER INFORMATION

The bot may cache publicly available career information associated with a linked WARDOGS identity, including:

- Wardog level
- Experience
- Cash
- Account worth
- Class progression
- Public leaderboard position

GIVEAWAY INFORMATION

When you participate in a giveaway, we may store:

- Discord user ID
- Discord username
- Linked Steam ID
- Linked WARDOGS name
- Giveaway registration status
- Giveaway entries
- Bonus entries
- Referral counts
- Verified referrals
- Winner status
- Giveaway event history

2. INFORMATION WE DO NOT INTENTIONALLY COLLECT

WPG Wardogs is not designed to collect:

- Passwords
- Discord passwords
- Steam passwords
- Credit card information
- Banking information
- Social Security numbers
- Private Discord direct messages
- Authentication credentials belonging to users

You should never send passwords, tokens, API keys, or other sensitive credentials to WPG Wardogs.

3. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

Information may be used to:

- Link Discord and gaming identities
- Display player profiles
- Display leaderboards
- Track WPG server progression
- Track kills, deaths, matches, wins, losses, and playtime
- Operate giveaways
- Verify referrals
- Prevent duplicate giveaway entries
- Prevent giveaway abuse
- Display server status
- Improve bot functionality
- Diagnose bugs
- Maintain WPG community features
- Protect the bot and community against abuse

4. LEGAL BASIS AND USER CHOICE

Account linking and giveaway registration are voluntary.

If you do not want your Steam or WARDOGS identity linked to Discord, do not use account-linking features.

Some bot features may not work without linked account information.

5. PUBLIC INFORMATION

Certain information displayed by WPG Wardogs may already be publicly available through game profiles, leaderboards, game servers, or other public services.

The bot may cache this information to improve performance and reduce repeated third-party requests.

Cached information may temporarily remain after the original public source changes.

6. DATA SHARING

WPG does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary for:

- Operating WPG Wardogs
- Operating WPG community features
- Preventing fraud or abuse
- Complying with legal obligations
- Protecting WPG, its users, or its systems

WPG may rely on third-party infrastructure providers to host or operate bot services.

Those providers may process information as necessary to provide their services.

7. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

WPG Wardogs may interact with services including:

- Discord
- Steam
- WARDOGS-related systems
- Public game-statistics services
- Game server hosting providers

Information handled directly by those services is subject to their own privacy policies.

WPG does not control how third-party services collect or use information outside WPG Wardogs.

8. DATA STORAGE

Bot information may be stored in local or hosted databases used by WPG Wardogs.

This may include account links, WPG statistics, giveaway records, referral records, and cached public statistics.

We take reasonable steps to protect stored information from unauthorized access.

However, no storage system can guarantee complete security.

9. DATA RETENTION

We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary for:

- WPG statistics
- Account linking
- Giveaway history
- Fraud prevention
- Bot operation
- Community administration
- Technical troubleshooting

Some WPG career statistics may be retained long-term because they represent permanent WPG server progression.

Giveaway history may be retained to prevent duplicate entries, investigate abuse, and maintain event records.

Cached public information may be refreshed, replaced, or deleted periodically.

10. DATA DELETION REQUESTS

You may request removal of information associated with your Discord account where reasonably possible.

A deletion request may include:

- Discord account link
- Steam link
- WARDOGS identity link
- Giveaway registration data
- Referral data
- Stored profile information

Some information may be retained where necessary for:

- Fraud prevention
- Security
- Legal obligations
- Giveaway integrity
- Administrative records

To request deletion, contact WPG using the contact information below.

Please provide enough information for us to identify the account involved.

11. DATA CORRECTION

If account-linking or profile information is incorrect, you may request that WPG correct or remove it.

Certain public statistics may need to be corrected at the original third-party source before WPG Wardogs can display updated information.

12. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

WPG Wardogs is intended for users permitted to use Discord under Discord's own age requirements.

We do not knowingly use the bot to collect sensitive personal information from children.

13. SECURITY

Reasonable technical and administrative measures may be used to protect WPG Wardogs data.

These may include:

- Restricted database access
- Limited administrative access
- Authentication controls
- Bot permission controls
- Backups
- Abuse monitoring

Users are responsible for protecting their own Discord and Steam accounts.

14. AUTOMATED PROCESSING

WPG Wardogs may automatically process information to:

- Calculate WPG ranks
- Calculate experience
- Calculate leaderboard placement
- Track match participation
- Calculate giveaway entries
- Calculate referral eligibility
- Select giveaway winners
- Update player statistics

These automated functions are used to operate community features.

15. INTERNATIONAL USERS

WPG Wardogs may be accessed by users in different countries.

By using the bot, you understand that information may be processed or stored in the United States or another location where WPG infrastructure operates.

16. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

This Privacy Policy may be updated as WPG Wardogs changes.

The current version will remain available at the public Privacy Policy URL used by the Discord application.

Continued use of WPG Wardogs after changes means the updated policy applies to future use.
